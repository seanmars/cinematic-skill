# Naturalistic Ambient

Summary: Soft interior or sky light with plausible direction; even with added lights, no deliberate lighting is visible.

## Narrative Function

- "Invisible" cinematography that keeps the audience focused on emotion rather than lighting technique.
- Suits quiet contemporary dramas and memory sequences.
- Unsuitable for graphic noir or theatrical glamour light.

## How It Works

- Start from a real window or overhead light, and add large soft sources or bounce along the same axis.
- Gentle contrast ratio, usually below 4:1; soft quality.
- Color matches the space (daylight through glass, warm tungsten at dusk); no hair light or kicker without spatial justification.

## When to Use

- Use: Quiet contemporary dramas, memory scenes.
- Avoid: Graphic noir styles or glamour lighting setups.

## Film Examples

- *Manchester by the Sea* (2016): New England interiors keep a believable winter daylight wrap.
- *Aftersun* (2022): Light in the holiday rooms feels as if found by chance.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Quiet morning in a small bedroom: soft, overcast window light from camera-right wraps the father and daughter with gentle directionality and a low contrast ratio. Believable soft shadows, natural room tone, no rim lights or theatrical accents.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Harsh rim light or unmodified direct sunlight appears, contradicting the brief → Align all lights with the window direction and soften until only a gentle sense of direction remains.
