# Hair Light

Summary: A small light from high behind on the crown of the hair, keeping dark hair from merging into a dark background.

## Narrative Function

- Keeps dark hair from disappearing into a dark background; a separation tool, not a beautifying device.
- Brings out highlights in hair texture in studio shots or close-ups.
- Unlike backlight, the face stays the key's job and is not relit.

## How It Works

- A small lamp placed high behind the subject, slightly opposite the key.
- Use a snoot or barn doors to confine the light to the hair and tops of the shoulders.
- Usually harder than the key so the hair sparkles; color temperature slightly warm or matched to the key; don't let it spill into the eyes.

## When to Use

- Use: Dark hair in dark interiors that needs separation; classical portraits and studio close-ups.
- Avoid: Broadly lighting the forehead and nose from the ceiling.
- Avoid: Hats, shaved heads, or when a full rear rim is already intended.

## Compared With Similar Techniques

| Technique | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| [Backlight](backlight.md) | Wraps the whole rear edge including shoulders; hair light reaches only the crown |
| [Rim Light](rim-light.md) | Follows the whole subject outline; hair light concentrates on the crown |

## Film Examples

- *Gentlemen Prefer Blondes* (1953): Classical studio portraits deliberately light the hair edge while the face still belongs to the key.
- *Vertigo* (1958): The same studio approach of building portraits with artificial hair light.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Studio close-up of a dark-haired woman against a near-black backdrop. A small snooted lamp high behind her sparkles only across the crown of her brown hair, while her face stays shaped by a soft key from the front-left. No halo around the head, no shine on the forehead.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Turning into a full backlight or a halo on the crown, with the forehead reflecting from above → Use a snoot so light lands only on the hair, and leave the face to the key.
- Light hair blown out as if the scalp were bleached → Specify hair color in the description and control intensity.
