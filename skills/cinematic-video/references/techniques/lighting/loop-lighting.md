# Loop Lighting

Summary: The key sits slightly off-axis; the nose shadow forms a small loop angled toward the mouth corner, not connecting with the cheek shadow.

## Narrative Function

- Shapes the face while staying approachable, suited to trustworthy characters.
- Less dramatic than Rembrandt, with clearer contours than butterfly.
- Looks like a natural portrait with "no special lighting."

## How It Works

- Key 30-45 degrees off-axis, slightly above eye line, medium softness, so the nose shadow forms a clean small loop.
- Fill from the opposite side, contrast ratio about 2:1 to 4:1.
- Color temperature follows the room (daylight or tungsten).

## When to Use

- Use: Natural portraits needing subtle dimension, interviews, seemingly unlit two-shots.
- Avoid: When symmetrical glamour or a dramatic cheek triangle is needed.

## Compared With Similar Techniques

| Technique | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| [Rembrandt](rembrandt.md) | Nose and cheek shadows connect into a triangle |
| [Butterfly Lighting](butterfly-lighting.md) | Centered key; butterfly-shaped shadow under the nose |
| [Split Lighting](split-lighting.md) | 90 degrees; the far cheek goes fully dark |
| [Bounce Light](bounce-light.md) | An oversized source wraps away the loop nose shadow |
| [Motivated Lighting](motivated-lighting.md) | Centered on source plausibility, not geometric pattern |

## Film Examples

- *Frost/Nixon* (2008): Interview-style lighting with a slightly off-axis key.
- *The Social Network* (2010): Often uses a slightly off-axis key, avoiding painterly triangle light.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Natural interview portrait: a soft key sits about 35 degrees off-axis and just above eye level, casting a small loop-shaped nose shadow that angles toward the corner of the mouth without touching the cheek shadow. Gentle 3:1 fill, warm tungsten room tone.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Confusing it with Rembrandt (connected cheek triangle) or butterfly (centered) → Adjust the key's off-axis angle so the nose shadow stays separate from the cheek shadow.
