# Dust Motes

Summary: Tiny dust particles drift slowly in a shaft of light, making time visible in still air.

## Narrative Function

- Conveys "stillness that is still alive," giving enclosed, quiet spaces (churches, bedrooms, barns) a sense of life.
- Makes time tangible through floating particles rather than empty silence.

## How It Works

- Create a hard shaft of light (window, blinds, HMI) and keep the surrounding air dark.
- Add fine particles and illuminate the beam with backlight or side light.
- Particles drift slowly on convection, not blown by wind.

## When to Use

- Use: Pauses, prayer, introspective moments in enclosed spaces.
- Avoid: Fast camera moves, strong wind, nightclubs, rooms filled entirely with haze, or sparks.

## Compared With Similar Techniques

| Technique | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| [Volumetric Light](../lighting/volumetric-light.md) | Creates the beam itself; dust motes are the countable inhabitants of the beam |
| [Smoke](smoke.md) | A continuous volume; dust motes are discrete, slow particles |
| [Sparks and Embers](sparks.md) | Glow and are short-lived |
| [Atmospheric Haze](haze.md) | Affects overall depth; dust motes exist only near the beam |

## Film Examples

- *The Assassination of Jesse James by the Coward Robert Ford* (2007): Window light fills the interior air with dust motes.
- *The Tree of Life* (2011): Fine particles hang in domestic and cosmic shafts of light.

## Prompt Examples

```text
A quiet old library in late afternoon: a single hard shaft of sunlight slants through a tall window into an otherwise dark room. Tiny glowing specks of dust drift lazily inside the beam, while an elderly woman sits motionless at its edge, reading.
```

## Common Mistakes

- The room is milky white with no defined beam → Use a hard beam with dark surroundings.
- Using haze or smoke instead of dust motes → Backlight countable, slowly drifting particles.
