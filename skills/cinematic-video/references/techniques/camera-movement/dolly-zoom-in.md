# Dolly Zoom In

Summary: The camera pushes forward while the lens widens; the subject holds its size but the background recedes, stretching the space.

## Narrative Function

- Creates an unsettling sense of "closing in," with the subject staying prominent while the environment distorts unnaturally.
- Conveys nausea or an unsettling realization during an approach.

## How It Works

- Dolly forward toward the subject along the lens axis while widening the focal length.
- Timing the two keeps the character's size constant; the wider focal length shrinks the background and extends it backward, exaggerating perspective.

## When to Use

- Use: When the approach should feel uncomfortable, e.g. realization, threat, psychological unease.
- Avoid: Using it as routine coverage; use sparingly.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Dolly zoom in: the camera pushes forward toward a young mother frozen at the playground gate while the lens widens at the same rate, her face holding its size as the swings and trees behind her shrink and rush away, the space stretching out behind her. ```
```

## Common Mistakes

- Only a plain push in or zoom → Must combine a forward dolly with a zoom out, with subject size held or growing while background perspective distorts.
