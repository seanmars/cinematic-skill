# Steadicam Shot

Summary: The operator walks wearing a mechanical stabilizing vest, the shot floating fluidly through connected interiors.

## Narrative Function

- Moves fluidly between connected interior spaces, like a continuous current of air.
- Removes footstep vibration while keeping a human-scale sense of presence.
- Crosses rooms without laying track, showing shifts in light and mood.

## How It Works

- The operator wears a vest and spring arm that mechanically isolate footstep vibration.
- The camera is mounted on a balanced sled.
- Rehearse the walking route in advance; the spring arm allows small rises and drops.

## When to Use

- Use: Long architectural paths linking multiple spaces, a single continuous route through doorways and between floors.
- Avoid: Static shots, scenes needing documentary-style handheld shake, or situations a regular tracking shot can handle.

## Compared With Similar Techniques

| Technique | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| [Gimbal](gimbal.md) | Motorized electronic stabilization, usually handheld, no vest needed |
| [Tracking](tracking.md) | Focus is on accompanying the subject's journey, not stabilization itself |
| [Oner](../time-and-motion/one-er.md) | Refers to long-take structure, unrelated to stabilization method |

## Film Examples

- *The Shining* (1980): the hallway sequences are a textbook example of Steadicam walking.
- *Goodfellas* (1990): enters the Copa nightclub through the back of house.
- *Boogie Nights* (1997): smooth, ground-level movement connects rooms into a social arena.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Steadicam shot: an operator walks the camera from a sunlit kitchen through a narrow hallway and into a candlelit dining room, gliding at chest height with no footstep bounce or tremor. The color temperature shifts from cool daylight to warm amber as the connected rooms unfold.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Adding handheld shake effects, or the weightless drift of an electronic gimbal → explicitly describe the operator walking, mechanical footstep isolation and passage through connected spaces.
