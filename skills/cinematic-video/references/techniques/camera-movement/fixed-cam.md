# Fixed Cam

Summary: Camera position, pan and tilt are fully locked; all motion in frame comes from the characters and environment.

## Narrative Function

- The camera refuses to help reframe, so blocking and mise-en-scène carry everything.
- Without reframing, duration becomes palpable.
- Best for scenes where the action is self-sufficient within the space.

## How It Works

- Mounted on solid support, usually at eye or chest height.
- Choose a lens with sufficient depth of field, commonly 35-50mm.
- Pan and tilt locked throughout.

## When to Use

- Use: When blocking carries the scene, e.g. rituals, household chores, wide comedic gags.
- Avoid: When dialogue or expressions need reframing to read clearly.

## Film Examples

- *Jeanne Dielman, 23 quai du Commerce, 1080 Bruxelles* (1975): Long, fixed compositions present domestic routine.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Fixed camera locked at chest height in a small kitchen, no pan, no tilt, no reframing for the entire shot. An old man slowly peels potatoes, walks out of frame to the sink and back again, while the composition stays completely still.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Subtle sneaky drift, or panning to keep a face centered → Use one composition only, fully lock pan and tilt, and let characters exit frame when needed.
