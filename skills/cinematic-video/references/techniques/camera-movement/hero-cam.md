# Hero Cam

Summary: A low-angle three-quarter tracking shot with rim light, presenting a comic-book mythic entrance at walking pace.

## Narrative Function

- Conveys inevitable, destined victory.
- Slow, unhurried movement plus a rim light that strongly separates the character from the background makes them seem destined.

## How It Works

- Tracks at hip or knee height, at a three-quarter angle, moving in one direction.
- A rim light or kicker light separates the character from the background.
- Keep walking pace (not a sprint); no crash zoom.

## When to Use

- Use: Mythic arrivals such as character entrances and title walk sequences.
- Avoid: Seated dialogue or static portraits.

## Compared With Similar Techniques

| Technique | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| [Low Angle](../camera-angles/low-angle.md) | About height only; can be static, no movement required |
| [Tracking Shot](tracking.md) | Follows a walker at any height, without the fixed rim-light-plus-walk formula |
| [Glam](../lighting/glam.md) | Beauty key light, no movement component |

## Film Examples

- *The Dark Knight* (2008): Mythic pacing, low-angle three-quarter composition and defining rim light.
- *Black Panther* (2018): Entrance walk sequences in the same grammar.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Hero cam: a low three-quarter tracking shot at knee height moves alongside a cloaked warrior walking steadily out of a smoky hangar, a strong blue rim light carving her silhouette from the dark. Unhurried walking pace, one direction, a sense of inevitable victory.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Shot as a static low-angle portrait, with the character standing still and no movement → Track at walking pace, in three-quarter composition, with rim light.
- Adding a crash zoom or sprint → Keep an unhurried walking rhythm.
