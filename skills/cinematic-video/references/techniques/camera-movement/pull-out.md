# Pull Out

Summary: The camera physically retreats from the subject; the subject stays in frame while the surroundings enter gradually through parallax.

## Narrative Function

- Reveals the surrounding environment while keeping the subject.
- Shows how a character or a moment is embedded in a larger context.
- Shifts from intimacy to environmental awareness without changing focal length.

## How It Works

- Physically retreat with a dolly, Steadicam or virtual camera.
- Focal length stays fixed throughout.
- As the camera retreats, new architectural and environmental elements enter from the frame edges; design the wide composition in advance so the reveal means something.

## When to Use

- Use: Placing an emotional moment back in its space.
- Use: Revealing the institution or environment that gives a feeling context.
- Avoid: Retreating into empty space; when aerial scale or a cold optical widening is needed.

## Film Examples

- *The Truman Show* (1998): retreats from an intimate frame to reveal the whole world is an artificial set.
- *The Player* (1992): uses retreats within a long camera move to show the studio system.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Gradual pull out from a close shot of a nurse sitting on the floor of a hospital corridor, fixed lens and no zoom. As the camera retreats, gurneys and doorframes slide past the frame edges with clear parallax, revealing the long, overcrowded ward around her while she stays visible.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Mistakenly made into a zoom out → physically retreat at a fixed focal length, letting architectural elements sweep past the frame edges with parallax.
