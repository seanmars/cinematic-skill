# Robo Arm

Summary: Executes a preprogrammed, repeatable camera path with mechanical precision and zero human shake.

## Narrative Function

- Guarantees identical motion across takes, enabling VFX compositing, product shoots and stunt alignment.
- Eliminates drift from manual operation.

## How It Works

- Use motion control or a six-axis robotic arm, programming XYZ coordinates and pan / tilt / roll parameters.
- Speed can be perfectly uniform or extremely fast.

## When to Use

- Use: Shots that must repeat precisely, e.g. VFX plates, product commercials, stunt geometry.
- Avoid: When a human touch and natural shake are wanted.

## Compared With Similar Techniques

| Technique | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| [360-Degree Orbit](orbit-360.md) | Orbit by walking or dolly, with natural variation; robo arm is a programmed curve |
| [Bolt Cam](bolt-cam.md) | Impact-style whip; robo arm focuses on a repeatable precise path |
| [Slow Zoom In](slow-zoom-in.md) | Adds pressure on a static subject, not an orbiting move |

## Film Examples

- Native to commercials and previs production, used for bottles, vehicles and stunt shots needing pixel-level alignment.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Robotic arm camera move: a perfectly smooth programmed path sweeps low around a sports car, rises over the hood and swings to a precise three-quarter front angle at uniform speed. Machine-precise, zero operator shake, every frame clean enough to match a VFX plate.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Done as a Steadicam orbit with slight sway and human acceleration and deceleration → explicitly specify a programmed, repeatable path with no human shake.
