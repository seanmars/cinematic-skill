# Eating Zoom

Summary: A fast snap zoom to the moment food enters the mouth, with utensils or the dish as the final foreground.

## Narrative Function

- The bite itself is the cut point, turning appetite into dramatic impact.
- Can lean comedic or sensual depending on the lighting.

## How It Works

- A snap zoom completed within a second, stopping and holding on the moment of the bite.
- No dolly.
- Needs visual evidence of food: steam, sheen, drips. The face can be partly shown; utensils must be prominent in the final frame.

## When to Use

- Use: When the bite is the key story event.
- Avoid: Empty plates, extreme wides of the restaurant, or whole-table coverage.

## Film Examples

- *Tampopo* (1985): The bite is the core of the scene.
- ASMR content borrows this grammar in a gentler form.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Eating zoom: a sudden snap zoom under one second lands on a chopstick lifting glossy, steaming ramen noodles into a man's open mouth, the chopsticks and dripping broth filling the foreground, then the frame holds on the bite.
```

## Common Mistakes

- No food visible in the final frame, or the whole restaurant still visible → Snap straight to the bite, with utensils as the final foreground, and hold the frame.
