# Natural Grade

Summary: No stylization; color faithfully renders place and time, so the audience believes the scene itself.

## Narrative Function

- Puts authenticity before aesthetics, so the audience believes the location rather than a "look".
- Suits stories where color must be honest; unsuitable when the palette needs to tell the story.

## How It Works

- White balance matches the actual location and time of day.
- Realistic skin tones: no teal faces, no plastic orange.
- Restrained saturation with no forced complementary colors; contrast follows the actual lighting conditions.

## When to Use

- Use: Near-documentary work, sunlit memory sequences.
- Avoid: When the scene needs a designed palette, feverish color or warm/cool separation.

## Film Examples

- *Aftersun* (2022): Keeps the authenticity of holiday light, letting emotional weight surface.
- *Timbuktu* (2014): Desert and village colors stay true to the locations.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Natural, honest color grade: white balance true to a late-morning seaside location, realistic skin with no orange or teal cast, walls and sky in their real colors. Restrained saturation and contrast that match the actual daylight, no stylized LUT look.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Slight teal-orange separation or LUT fading creates a "fake natural" look with teal shadows and plastic skin → keep skin faithful, walls and sky true to the location.
- Unconsciously adding complementary color casts → remove the casts and keep saturation restrained.
