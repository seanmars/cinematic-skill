# Warm Amber

Summary: The whole world is bathed in honey-colored warm light, yet every color stays intact.

## Narrative Function

- Creates a hospitable, homely mood with honey-colored interiors.
- Builds memory, safety or invitation in the midtones.
- Unlike sepia (removes saturation) and tungsten balance (a white balance fact), it is an overall warm bias.

## How It Works

- Shift midtones warm, to about 2800K-4000K.
- Use practicals such as desk lamps and candles as the main sources.
- Reduce green cast to keep skin from turning yellow; unless deliberately overexposing, don't let whites clip.

## When to Use

- Use: Domestic spaces, nostalgic moments, warm venues (hotel corridors, late-night kitchens).
- Avoid: Crime scenes needing coldness, old-photo-style flashbacks, ruthless narratives.

## Compared With Similar Techniques

| Technique | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| [Sepia](sepia.md) | A brown-gold scale replaces color; warm amber keeps full color |
| [Golden Hour](../lighting/golden-hour.md) | Light shot at a specific time of day, not a grade |
| [Tungsten Balance](tungsten-balance.md) | A white balance correction, not an overall honey bias |
| [Teal and Orange](teal-orange.md) | Warm/cool separation; warm amber is unified warmth |
| [Candlelight](../lighting/candlelight.md) | That is a light source; warm amber is an overall grade |

## Film Examples

- *Amélie* (2001): Warm practicals make Paris feel like a cozy hearth.
- *The Grand Budapest Hotel* (2014): Designed warm tones and pastels convey hospitality.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Warm amber grade: the whole room bathed in honey-colored tungsten light from table lamps and candles, cozy golden midtones, natural healthy skin. Full color is preserved, walls and clothing keep their own hues, no sepia wash and no cool teal contrast.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Confused with sepia, or turned into teal-orange warm/cool separation → keep full saturation and use fixtures as mood anchors.
- Jaundiced yellow skin → reduce the green cast.
