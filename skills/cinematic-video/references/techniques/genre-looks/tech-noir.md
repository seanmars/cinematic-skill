# Tech Noir

Summary: Film noir's shadowy morality moved into a rainy digital city at night, with screens and LEDs replacing streetlamps as the key light.

## Narrative Function

- Fuses classic noir's moral shadows with sci-fi aesthetics.
- Guilt and investigation unfold in a rain-soaked digital metropolis, where technological light sources are bound up with crime.

## How It Works

- Maintain noir's hard-light contrast and unfilled shadows.
- Replace tungsten with LED displays and screens as the motivated key; add smoke or rain for atmospheric depth.
- 35-40mm anamorphic, night exteriors; first shoot figures tiny beneath signage, then cut to screen-lit close-ups.

## When to Use

- Use: When the future city is both crime scene and character, and technological light sources tie into guilt.
- Avoid: Clean utopian interfaces.
- Avoid: Contemporary noir without speculative design elements (use Neo-Noir).

## Compared With Similar Techniques

| Technique | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| [Neo-Noir](neo-noir.md) | Color noir, with no need for future technology |
| [Dystopian](dystopian.md) | Often daytime, with the state as adversary; not necessarily focused on crime |
| [Film Noir](film-noir.md) | The monochrome ancestor, with no technological light sources |

## Film Examples

- *Blade Runner* (1982): A detective story amid rain, neon and a mechanized city.
- *The Terminator* (1984): Tech-noir streets and nightclubs as crime settings.
- *Ghost in the Shell* (1995): Screens, rain and an investigation that explores identity.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Tech noir night exterior: a small figure in a trench coat stands in heavy rain beneath towering corporate skyscrapers covered in giant video screens, then cut to a close-up where a flickering holographic display is the only hard key on her face. Unfilled shadows, drifting smoke, 35mm anamorphic.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Confusing it with Neo-Noir or generic dystopia, missing screen light sources → Add displays as the key light.
- Daytime scenes appear → Switch to rainy nights, high-rises and blocking that stresses isolation.
