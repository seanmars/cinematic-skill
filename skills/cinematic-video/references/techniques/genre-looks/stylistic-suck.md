# Stylistic Suck

Summary: Deliberately chooses amateur, ugly camcorder image quality as the auteur's voice, not as an accident or a character's point of view.

## Narrative Function

- Ugliness itself is the film's argument: conveying poverty, comedy, cruelty or a rejection of conventional beauty.
- Auto-exposure jumps, overshooting zooms and blown highlights reinforce thematic honesty over polish.

## How It Works

- Emulate home camcorder / VHS limits: auto-gain hunting, runaway zooms, rolling shutter.
- Leave wrong white balance uncorrected; clumsy composition.
- Refuse grading fixes or strategic inserts that would weaken this commitment.

## When to Use

- Use: When polish would betray the nature of the story.
- Avoid: Genuinely incompetent filmmaking.
- Avoid: Adding timestamps or an in-story camera unless the narrative requires it.

## Film Examples

- *The Blair Witch Project* (1999): Where amateur optics and found footage intersect.
- *Trash Humpers* (2009): VHS degradation as a conceptual work.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Deliberately ugly camcorder aesthetic: a cheap VHS-style shot of three people dancing awkwardly in a suburban parking lot at dusk, the zoom lurching past its target, auto-gain pumping, highlights blown out and a sickly green white balance left uncorrected. Off-center clumsy framing, no timestamp overlay.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Adding timestamps or alternate angles, drifting into found footage → Keep a consistent "ugly omniscient" perspective.
- If the story truly needs an in-story camera → Switch fully to Found Footage.
