# Arthouse

Summary: Locked-off camera, natural light, generous empty space and long holds put time and contemplation ahead of visual polish.

## Narrative Function

- Leaves room for reflection, letting "boredom" become a gateway to deeper engagement.
- The frame is deliberately incomplete; the extra air is meaningful, not a composition error.

## How It Works

- Locked-off on a tripod, 35mm or 50mm spherical lenses.
- Natural or window light as key, minimal fill, no rim light unless motivated by the environment.
- Unusual headroom, heavy negative space, muted natural color, and long takes to strengthen the sense of lived time.

## When to Use

- Use: Scenes that need contemplative space and temporal depth.
- Avoid: Action sequences, billboard-style composition.
- Avoid: Vérité-style movement or centered tableau composition.

## Film Examples

- *Toni Erdmann* (2016): Flat lighting, the duration of social situations, unflattering composition.
- *Roma* (2018): 65mm black and white, patient observation.
- *Drive My Car* (2021): Long holds inside cars and interiors carry grief.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Arthouse style: a locked-off 35mm frame of an elderly man sitting alone at the far lower edge of a plain kitchen, large empty wall above him, flat grey window light as the only source. Muted natural colors, no rim light, the shot simply holds as nothing happens for a long time.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Adding rim light, centered composition or heroic lighting → Remove supplemental lighting.
- Composition too tidy → Add headroom and allow compositional awkwardness.
