# Screen in Screen

Summary: A screen or display is embedded in the frame; the audience watches "the person watching," or an interface takes over the whole frame.

## Narrative Function

- Nested displays establish power relations: the person on screen is already controlled by someone else (director, hacker, those in power).
- The audience watches the watcher, or the interface itself becomes the camera.
- Success depends on legible text and cursors; otherwise it is meaningless set dressing.

## How It Works

- Design a stable timeline and legible composition for the inner frame.
- Produce or shoot the inner image as a separate asset, then composite it into the screen with correct perspective, refresh and reflections.
- Build UI deterministically; don't rely on generators that produce unstable text.

## When to Use

- Use: when "mediated viewing" is thematically meaningful, e.g. surveillance, livestreams, laptop investigations, projections, god's-eye monitoring.
- Avoid: screen content unrelated to the plot, or illegible visual noise.

## Film Examples

- *Searching* (2018): The whole investigation unfolds through screens and digital interfaces.
- *Unfriended* (2014): Fear escalates through computer interfaces and video calls.
- *Sherlock Jr.* (1924): The projectionist walks straight into the movie screen.
- *The Stendhal Syndrome* (1996): The protagonist's perception pulls her into paintings.
- *Paprika* (2006): Screens become passages between dream and reality.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Screen in screen: over the shoulder of a tired detective in a dark room, a laptop monitor shows a crisp security camera feed of an empty parking lot with a clearly readable timestamp. Correct monitor perspective, soft screen glow on his face, faint reflection of his silhouette on the glass.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Blurry TVs, fake split screens, unreadable text, melting UI, or collaged images with no physical device → treat the inner frame as a carefully designed insert, use real typefaces, and place screens in a physically plausible way.
