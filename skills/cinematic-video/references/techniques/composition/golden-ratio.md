# Golden Ratio

Summary: Arrange the frame by a ratio of about 1:1.618 and a spiral path so the eye flows naturally along the curve to the subject.

## Narrative Function

- Creates an understated sense of order; the frame looks "inevitable" without revealing the math behind it.
- The eye travels an invisible curve past secondary elements instead of jumping between grid points.

## How It Works

- Place dividing lines at about 38% and 62%, further inward than the 33% of the rule of thirds.
- Arrange masses along a logarithmic spiral starting from one corner; the end of the spiral is the eye's destination.
- A medium focal length (about 40-65mm) is recommended to preserve proportions; ultra-wide lenses distort the relationships.

## When to Use

- Use: deliberately designed, painterly scenes, e.g. staged interior portraits, landscapes with architectural flow.
- Avoid: fast action, comedic blocking, general coverage.
- Avoid: when the scene has no elements that can form a spiral path.

## Compared With Similar Techniques

| Technique | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| [Rule of Thirds](rule-of-thirds.md) | 33% divisions, coarser, a simple default |
| [Centered Composition](centered-composition.md) | Subject fixed on the central axis, with no spiral progression of the eye |
| [Central Framing](central-framing.md) | Subject centered like a crosshair; the frame has no flow |

## Film Examples

- *Barry Lyndon* (1975): Figures and furniture arranged in harmonious proportion within interiors.
- *The Tree of Life* (2011): Landscape, light and figures together form a spiraling eye path.
- *Portrait of a Lady on Fire* (2019): Faces and firelight form slow curves.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Golden ratio spiral composition: the curve begins at a window in the upper-right corner, sweeps through a table and chair, and ends on a woman's face placed at the 62% point of the frame. Painterly interior, soft natural light, 50mm lens, calm and balanced.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Just overlaying a spiral on a rule-of-thirds composition (subject still at 33%, masses not redistributed) → move the main mass toward the 62% point and arrange real secondary elements into a traceable eye path.
