# Marble

Summary: The subject looks carved into a marble statue, with stone veining, a polished surface and cool, museum-like light.

## Narrative Function

- Treats the subject as carved stone, conveying mythic or monumental themes.
- Creates cold, eternal beauty; the point is stone texture (veining, polish), not just pale desaturation.

## How It Works

- Eye level, 50-85mm, locked shot.
- Soft north skylight or gallery skylight.
- Key: Visible stone veining, a polished surface and a "museum-cool" color temperature, so the skin reads as mineral.

## When to Use

- Use: Mythic subjects, high fashion, characters presented as monuments, or moments when a character becomes eternal in the story.
- Avoid: Pale beauty portraits with no stone veining; the result is shallow and inaccurate.

## Film Examples

- *The Great Beauty* (2013): Roman stone builds a moral atmosphere around the characters.
- *The Belly of an Architect* (1987): Marble is both an architectural element and a character's obsession.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Bust-length portrait of a dancer transformed into living Carrara marble, 85mm eye-level, locked frame, soft cool north skylight like a museum gallery. Gray veins run across her polished cheek and shoulders, faint sheen on the stone surface, her chest barely rising as if the statue breathes, no CG plastic smoothness, no graffiti.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Becomes a pale beauty shot, or drifts into a plastic 3D render → Emphasize stone veining, a polished surface, cool gallery light and sculptural presence.
