# Mighty Fighter

Summary: A real person strikes a victory pose like arcade cabinet art, with saturated color and hard rim light, like a fighting-game character select screen.

## Narrative Function

- Conveys victory and a heroic entrance through game-character presentation.
- Suits title cards and comic-style action beats.

## How It Works

- Low three-quarter angle, 24-35mm, locked shot.
- Strong rim light paired with a saturated, crisp key light.
- A single figure, with a flat graphic stage as the background rather than a realistic setting.
- No motion blur.

## When to Use

- Use: Entrance sequences, title cards, comic action moments that need a "victory pose".
- Avoid: Quiet confessions, weary realism, documentary-style sequences.

## Film Examples

- *Street Fighter* (1994): The opening presents champion characters in arcade-art poses.
- *Kung Fury* (2015): Uses exaggerated champion posing and retro colors.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Low three-quarter locked-off shot at 28mm: a boxer in a wide fighting stance, fists raised, against a flat graphic arena backdrop of bold color bands. Hard cyan rim light and a saturated magenta key, toy-box vivid colors, crisp edges, zero motion blur, still clearly a real sweating human.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Just an ordinary low angle, lacking toy-box saturated color → Boost color vibrancy and rim light.
- The figure starts to look like a plastic doll → Keep real human texture and widen the stance.
