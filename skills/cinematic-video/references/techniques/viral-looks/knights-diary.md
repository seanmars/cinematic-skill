# Knight's Diary

Summary: The frame looks like an illuminated manuscript page, with gold-leaf highlights over mud, iron armor and weary faces.

## Narrative Function

- Presents quests, confessions or crusades with period weight, treating the frame as a medieval manuscript page.
- Gold-leaf highlights combined with weathered, real materials, not a clean fantasy aesthetic.

## How It Works

- Eye level, 35-50mm, locked shot.
- Hard or semi-hard daylight so highlights read as gold leaf.
- Emphasize texture: mud on cloth, heavy iron, visible wear.
- A single clear figure as the focal point; avoid polished costume steel armor.

## When to Use

- Use: Quest imagery, moments of confession, period portraits conveying exhaustion.
- Avoid: Clean LARP, shiny theme-park knights, touristy medieval styling.

## Film Examples

- *The Green Knight* (2021): Mud, metal and an eerie golden light surround a weary seeker.
- *A Knight's Tale* (2001): Quiet frames of iron armor and ritual before the tone shifts.

## Prompt Examples

```text
A mud-spattered knight sits beside his horse at eye level, 35mm locked-off, hard afternoon sun. Highlights on his dented iron armor and the horse's bridle glow like burnished gold leaf, dirty wool cloak, exhausted face, the whole frame composed like an illuminated manuscript page, no polished chrome.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Fantasy-stock chrome steel and clean textures → Emphasize gold leaf, the weight of iron, mud and exhaustion.
- Drifting into Agamemnon's bruised dusk tones → Return to gold-leaf highlights and manuscript composition.
- Creating the gold with a filter → The gold must appear on the materials in the highlights.
