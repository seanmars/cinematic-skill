# Puffin Ride

Summary: Someone rides a puffin with a straight face, "commuting" among majestic coastal cliffs.

## Narrative Function

- Used for comic sequences and dreams, presenting an impossible sightseeing experience.
- The joke comes from treating a flying seabird as an absurd commuter vehicle against a dramatic coastal backdrop.

## How It Works

- Wide 24-35mm, at rider height or slightly lower.
- The person rides directly on the puffin; North Atlantic coastal light, overcast or sunny.
- The puffin's orange beak must be clearly recognizable; the rider is deadpan and earnest.

## When to Use

- Use: Comic commuting fantasies, impossible cliff sightseeing.
- Avoid: Wildlife documentaries that respect puffin habitat; puffins appearing only in the background rather than as the mount.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Wide 28mm shot at rider height along towering sea cliffs in overcast North Atlantic light: a hiker in a yellow rain jacket sits astride an oversized puffin gliding over crashing surf, its bright orange beak and white face clearly visible, ocean spray misting the frame, the rider completely deadpan, no branding.
```

## Common Mistakes

- The puffin is beside the hiker rather than ridden, or it actually comes out as a penguin → Ensure a clear riding posture and add coastal surf.
