# Sketch

Summary: The subject is drawn as a pencil or ink sketch, with hatched shadows, paper-white highlights and unfinished searching lines clearly visible.

## Narrative Function

- Conveys process, memory and "a story still happening".
- The unfinished quality emphasizes hand-drawn exploration rather than a polished result; photographic structure remains underneath.

## How It Works

- Medium focal length 50-85mm, locked shot.
- Paper white serves as highlights, with pencil or ink lines and shadows rendered in hatching.
- Key: Keep the repeated, searching lines; don't resolve it into a finished illustration.

## When to Use

- Use: Depicting the creative process, memory, or a narrative still unfolding.
- Avoid: When polished commercial feel, finished illustration or full animation is needed.

## Film Examples

- *Waltz with Bashir* (2008): Uses hand-drawn lines as the primary imagery.
- *Loving Vincent* (2017): Shows preliminary studies before the oil paintings.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Portrait of a violinist mid-performance, 70mm, locked frame, rendered as a loose graphite sketch on off-white paper. Searching, overlapping contour lines, cross-hatching only in the shadows, paper left bare for highlights, edges fading out unfinished, the pose still grounded in a real photographic structure.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Drawn too completely, becoming a clean vector graphic or oil painting → Emphasize hatching, bare paper, searching contours and unresolved edges.
