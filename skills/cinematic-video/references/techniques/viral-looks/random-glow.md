# Random Glow

Summary: Sourceless, irregular glows and colored orbs appear at random like analog film light leaks, creating a dream or a faded memory.

## Narrative Function

- Evokes dreams, nightlife or memories like degraded film.
- Uses "unmotivated" light to create unease, deliberately breaking the logic that every light needs a source.

## How It Works

- Locked shot, 35-50mm, face kept clear.
- Irregular glows and colored orbs appear for no reason, simulating analog light leaks.
- Glows must be uneven; they cannot evenly cover the entire frame.

## When to Use

- Use: Dream sequences, nightclub scenes, analog horror style, nostalgic degraded memories.
- Avoid: Medical or clinical situations that need clear expository lighting; also don't make the effect uniform.

## Prompt Examples

```text
A girl sits on a bed in a dark bedroom, 50mm static shot, her face clearly lit and readable. Random soft orbs of amber, magenta and teal bloom and fade at irregular spots around her with no visible source, like analog film leaks, uneven and patchy, with patches of clean unaffected skin.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Glow spreads evenly or washes out too much, covering the face → Keep orbs uneven, leave clean areas of skin, and keep the subject readable.
