# Dolphin Ride

Summary: Someone rides a dolphin's back through the waves, an impossible vacation presented with documentary seriousness.

## Narrative Function

- Shoots an absurd vacation fantasy as fact.
- The rider looks earnest or happy, never mugging for the camera; the dolphin is transportation.

## How It Works

- Wide 24-35mm, with an honest, level horizon.
- Camera at the rider's height or slightly above the water.
- Bright sunlight, visible water reflections and spray.
- The rider is clearly recognizable and rises and falls with the dolphin's motion; no logos or staged swimming poses.

## When to Use

- Use: Comic vacation fantasies, dreamlike travel sequences.
- Avoid: Wildlife documentaries that require respect for animals, or just splashing beside a dolphin without actually riding it.

## Film Examples

- *Big Miracle* (2012): Presents marine life with honest water surfaces and animal bodies.
- The riding meme itself was popularized by online AI videos.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Wide 28mm shot just above the waterline on a bright tropical morning: a man in a snorkel mask sits astride a leaping dolphin, gripping its dorsal fin, spray and sun glitter exploding around them, level horizon, his face fully committed and thrilled, no logos, no winking at the camera.
```

## Common Mistakes

- The person swims beside the dolphin, or winks cutely at the camera → Must actually ride on its back, fully committed.
- Static image → Keep the dolphin's motion readable and spray constantly flying.
