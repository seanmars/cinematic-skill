# 3D Render

Summary: The subject looks like a flawless digital product computed in CG software, with wraparound HDRI lighting, immaculate shader skin and an infinite backdrop.

## Narrative Function

- Presents the subject as a polished digital commodity rather than organic life.
- Used for fashion-as-product, advertising, or characters deliberately designed to be non-human.

## How It Works

- Locked 50-85mm shot, eye level or a slight turntable-style three-quarter angle.
- Wraparound HDRI lighting, perfectly even subsurface scattering (SSS), an infinite cyclorama backdrop.
- Clean contact shadows with no weathering; remove film grain and dirty optical artifacts.

## When to Use

- Use: Ads treating people as products, characters with a non-organic feel, fashion visualization.
- Avoid: Period dramas and naturalistic stories that need realism.

## Compared With Similar Techniques

| Technique | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| [Photogrammetry](../effects/photogrammetry.md) | Scans the real world and keeps imperfections; 3D Render is deliberately sterile |
| [Product](../framing/product.md) | Lighting discipline for objects; 3D Render applies it to people |
| [Action Figure](action-figure.md) | Plastic toy material, not shader skin |
| [Marble](marble.md) | Stone texture, not computed lighting |

## Film Examples

- *The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (2002): Gollum's motion-capture performance.
- *Rise of the Planet of the Apes* (2011): Caesar's nuanced emotional arc.
- *Avatar* (2009): Consistent visual integration of live actors and virtual characters.

## Prompt Examples

```text
A fashion model rendered as a pristine CG character on an infinite white cyclorama, 70mm eye-level, slight turntable three-quarter angle, locked camera. Even studio HDRI lighting wraps around flawless subsurface-scattered skin, a soft clean contact shadow under her feet, zero film grain, zero dust, perfectly computed surfaces.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Residual photographic grain or scanned surface flaws → Use shader textures, clean global illumination and an infinite backdrop to eliminate all film noise.
