# Modern

Summary: A single figure within minimalist architecture of vast glass and geometric emptiness, where the building itself becomes the star.

## Narrative Function

- Creates isolation and luxury through the overwhelming presence of architecture.
- Minimal objects and austere geometry convey wealth, alienation, or showroom-like formal conversations.

## How It Works

- Locked shot, eye level or slightly high, 35mm.
- One figure placed among broad glass planes and geometric voids.
- Daylight through glass is the main light source, cutting in like a knife edge.
- Restrained palette: two neutrals, optionally with a touch of warm wood as an accent.

## When to Use

- Use: Depicting alienation, affluence or cold conversation scenes.
- Avoid: Cluttered environments, string lights, contemporary fashion aesthetics; an ordinary furnished apartment won't achieve the effect, so it must be deliberately emptied.

## Film Examples

- *Playtime* (1967): Architecture and people side by side become thematic elements.
- *The International* (2009): Architecture dominates the frame, symbolizing power and alienation.

## Prompt Examples

```text
A lone woman in a charcoal coat stands in a vast glass-walled concrete pavilion, 35mm eye-level, locked-off. Knife-edge afternoon daylight slices through the floor-to-ceiling panes, the room nearly empty except for one low walnut bench, palette of cool gray and white, the architecture swallowing her in designed emptiness.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Cluttered interior with too many objects → Ruthlessly clear out objects, emphasize glass and planes, and place only one person within.
