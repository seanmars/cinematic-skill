# 35mm Moderate Wide

Summary: "The storytelling focal length": faces look natural while meaningful room context stays visible.

## Narrative Function

- Shoots dialogue and scenes with balanced perspective; faces look natural while the environment is established.
- Less subjective commentary than wider lenses, but more environmental information than 50mm.

## How It Works

- Place the camera at conversational distance, roughly like someone talking across a table.
- Close to a normal focal length on Super 35; slightly wide on full frame.
- Unless deliberately tilted, keep verticals straight.

## When to Use

- Use: General narrative coverage needing human scale with a sense of space, such as a dinner-table two-shot or blocking through a doorway.
- Avoid: When the location should visually dominate the frame.
- Avoid: When the narrative focus is isolating the subject.

- *Moonlight* (2016): Interior face coverage still keeps Miami's environmental presence.
- *Manchester by the Sea* (2016): New England interiors keep people and place in balance.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Shot on a 35mm lens at conversational distance: two siblings talk across a small kitchen table, their faces natural in proportion while the worn cabinets and window behind them remain readable. Straight verticals, soft window light, grounded naturalistic drama.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Defaulting too tight (like 50mm) or too stretched (like 24mm) → keep conversational distance, balance face and setting, and control perspective.
