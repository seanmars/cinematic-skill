# Spherical

Summary: Modern standard capture optics: no squeeze, round bokeh, straight edges, a clean sharp image.

## Narrative Function

- Provides control and sharpness; the default choice for modern capture.
- Round bokeh and no squeeze distortion suit scenes that don't want optical artifacts.
- Used when the narrative relies on clarity rather than anamorphic character.

## How It Works

- Choose spherical primes or zooms.
- Rotationally symmetric optical design records at the actual display ratio, needing no desqueeze in post.
- Bokeh shape depends on aperture blade design (round aperture → round highlights).

## When to Use

- Use: VFX plates, shots needing compositing, general coverage needing sharpness.
- Avoid: When the narrative needs oval highlights and horizontal flares.
- Avoid: Mislabeling a cropped spherical image as anamorphic.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Shot on a spherical 40mm prime: a chef plates a dish in a bright modern kitchen, crisp clean detail, round circular bokeh from the pendant lights behind her, straight rectilinear edges. No anamorphic streaks, no oval highlights, clean contemporary look.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Labeling a cropped image with round bokeh as anamorphic → confirm straight edges, round bokeh, no squeeze, and state the actual focal length.
