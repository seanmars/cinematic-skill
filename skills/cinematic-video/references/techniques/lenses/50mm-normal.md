# 50mm Normal

Summary: A neutral angle of view close to the human eye; facial proportions and spatial scale look natural, and the lens seems invisible.

## Narrative Function

- Keeps the viewing experience unobtrusive; the lens's presence disappears.
- Maintains natural facial proportions and room scale; suits scenes driven by dialogue and intimate moments.

## How It Works

- Place the camera at conversational distance.
- On full frame it is the classic neutral focal length, neither magnifying nor distorting spatial relationships.
- Moderate depth of field, unless using a wide aperture or very close distance.

## When to Use

- Use: Scenes valuing honest observation over stylized composition, such as interviews, intimate dialogue, direct character coverage.
- Avoid: When the space itself needs dramatic emphasis.
- Avoid: When the narrative needs compression or isolation.

## Film Examples

- Classical coverage often uses 50mm for singles and over-the-shoulder shots, keeping the optics invisible and letting performance and blocking carry the scene.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Shot on a 50mm normal lens at conversational distance: a medium close-up of an elderly man telling a story at his kitchen table, natural facial proportions, the room softly present behind him without compression or distortion. Neutral, honest, unobtrusive camera.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Treating a 35mm or 85mm image as 50mm: too much of the room revealed, or the background noticeably compressed → keep conversational distance, preserve natural facial scale, and let the environment be present without stealing the scene.
