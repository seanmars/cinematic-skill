# Product

Summary: A commercial portrait that treats the object as the star, with designed lighting making its form, material and reflections clearly readable.

## Narrative Function

- The object is the hero, not a story detail.
- Prioritizes form, material and reflections.
- The background usually disappears into a seamless sweep or solid-color void to avoid visual competition.

## How It Works

- Choose a three-quarter hero angle or a straight-on front view.
- A large soft source wraps the object in clean highlights; flags and bounce cards control stray specular on reflective materials.
- Avoid macro unless texture is the point; add a slow rotation when multiple sides need showing.

## When to Use

- Use: Catalog-style product showcases (watches, phones, bottles).
- Avoid: When the object is a story element in a scene and needs environmental context.
- Avoid: Story props that should be "discovered" rather than "displayed".

## Film Examples

- Commercials and Apple keynote videos: isolated objects, clean surfaces, deliberately designed highlights.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Product shot of a matte black wireless earbud case at a three-quarter hero angle on a seamless charcoal sweep. Large soft overhead source creates one clean highlight along the lid edge, black flags cut stray reflections, slow 30-degree turntable rotation.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Using insert-style messy practical light, or letting chrome / glass produce uncontrolled reflections → Isolate the object, design the highlights deliberately and establish a clear hero angle.
