# Masking

Summary: The frame is blocked by a hard-edged shape, revealing the image only through an opening shaped like binoculars, a keyhole and so on.

## Narrative Function

- Lets the audience see what a character sees through an object.
- Surroundings are fully black or opaque, leaving only the image inside the shape, visualizing the act of "looking" itself.

## How It Works

- Place a physical mask in front of the lens (binoculars, paper cutout, iris plate), or add a hard-edged matte during optical printing.
- Compose the subject within the shape, not the whole sensor.
- Light the interior as a real scene; the mask itself needs no separate lighting.

## When to Use

- Use: Voyeurism, surveillance, or title sequences "seen through an object."
- Avoid: When you only want a tighter composition or a different aspect ratio.
- Avoid: When the container itself needs to widen (use ratio switch), or architecture already frames the shot naturally (use frame within frame).

## Compared With Similar Techniques

| Technique | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| [Frame within Frame](../composition/frame-in-frame.md) | Uses existing architectural elements to frame; masking imposes a shape that represents "looking" |
| [Iris](../editing/iris.md) | A transition effect; masking is a persistent viewing shape |
| [Ratio Switch](ratio-switch.md) | Changes the outer frame ratio; masking cuts a shape within a stable outer frame |

## Film Examples

- *Kill Bill: Vol. 2* (2004): Viewed through coffin cracks and narrow graphic holes.
- *The Grand Budapest Hotel* (2014): Frames shots with deliberately designed window shapes.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Point-of-view through a pair of binoculars: two overlapping hard-edged circles reveal a woman on a distant balcony, and everything outside the circles is solid black. The interior is lit like a normal daytime scene, with a slight drift as if held by hand.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Soft, dim edges that look like a vignette → keep a hard-edged shape and a fully opaque surround.
- The whole frame widens, like a ratio switch → keep the shape fixed within the frame with real imagery inside.
