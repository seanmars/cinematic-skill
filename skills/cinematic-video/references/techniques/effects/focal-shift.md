# Focal Shift

Summary: Without cutting, the sharp focal plane slowly moves from foreground to background, and attention moves with it.

## Narrative Function

- Moves the audience's attention from one subject to another without cutting.
- When the location stays the same but importance changes, the blur itself becomes the carrier of meaning.

## How It Works

- Set two marked distances and use a shallow depth of field (about 50-85mm, T2 or wider).
- Use a follow focus or remote focus motor to move the focal plane from point A to point B.
- Lock off the camera or move it slowly; the focus move is the shot's main event.
- Lens breathing may slightly magnify the image during the focus pull.

## When to Use

- Use: A foreground object (wine glass, weapon, listener) hands focus over to a character who must respond.
- Avoid: Decorative use on a single face with no second plane.
- Avoid: When both foreground and background must be sharp.

## Compared With Similar Techniques

| Technique | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| [Rack Focus](rack-focus.md) | A standard two-point focus pull (about 2 seconds) with a static composition; focal shift treats the movement of the focal plane itself as the event |
| [Shallow Focus](shallow-focus.md) | A static shallow depth-of-field state; focal shift is the movement between two states |

## Film Examples

- *The Graduate* (1967): Selective focus distributes attention between Benjamin and the people around him.
- *There Will Be Blood* (2007): Shifts attention without cutting in stark interior spaces.

## Prompt Examples

```text
Shallow depth of field on an 85mm lens: a revolver on the table in the foreground is sharp while a man in the background is a soft blur. Without any camera movement, the plane of focus glides slowly from the gun to the man's face, leaving the weapon to melt into bokeh.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Both foreground and background planes sharp at once → keep a shallow depth of field so only one plane is sharp.
- Facial detail warps during the focus pull → set two clear focus marks and move the focal plane cleanly.
