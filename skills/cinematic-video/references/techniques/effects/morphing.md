# Morphing

Summary: One form continuously becomes another through aligned feature points and geometric interpolation, rather than a fade.

## Narrative Function

- Presents a change of identity or form as a correspondence between feature points.
- "Becoming" is a continuous change of contour, not a cut or dissolve.
- Eyes and pose stay aligned; only the features evolve.

## How It Works

- Lock the pose, eyeline and key features at both endpoints.
- Align feature points (eye to eye, mouth to mouth, contour to contour).
- Separate foreground from background so the whole room doesn't warp along.
- Deliberately design the in-between states rather than a melting blob; interpolate geometry continuously throughout.

## When to Use

- Use: One form must become another within a few seconds in a continuous shot, with alignable features at both ends.
- Avoid: Two shots with no shared feature points.
- Avoid: When the person stays the same and only the room changes, or a simple dissolve is enough.

## Compared With Similar Techniques

| Technique | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| [Morph](match-morph.md) | Uses the morph as a cut point, expressing unstable identity |
| [Transformation](transformation.md) | The change itself is the whole scene, driven by makeup or set changes rather than feature-point geometry |
| Dissolve | Only an opacity transition; shape doesn't change |

## Film Examples

- *Terminator 2: Judgment Day* (1991): Feature-aligned interpolated morphs between liquid metal and the human body.
- *Black or White* (1991, Michael Jackson music video): Different faces transition continuously via aligned feature points.
- *Willow* (1988): Interpolated morph sequence between creature forms.

## Prompt Examples

```text
A continuous face morph: an old woman's face smoothly becomes a young girl's face, eyes and mouth staying locked in the same position while bone structure, skin and hair reshape between them. Fixed frontal head pose, even lighting on both endpoints, the background untouched.
```

## Common Mistakes

- Done as an opacity dissolve with no shape change → make feature points truly move and contours change continuously.
- The face liquefies without design → align pose and lighting at both ends and design clear in-between states.
